The Atelier

The back office, for someone who'd rather be making things.

Peonies & Pinot is a Toronto studio doing floral design and wine experiences. Its owner is analog and wants nothing to do with software. The Atelier does the typing, and she just says yes.

Open the Atelier → Sign-in needed. Ask Rama for the demo login.

How it works

  1. QuoteAfter a first meeting she talks through her notes. Leo, the built-in assistant, turns them into a quote ready to send.
  2. ReceiptsSnap a photo, and each cost lands on the right job.
  3. InvoiceThree screens: check it, check the total, send.

Meet Leo

Leo is the Atelier's assistant. She can talk to him, or type. In hands-free mode he listens and answers on his own, so she can use him while driving or in gardening gloves.

  • Drafts the writing. He turns notes into a spec and a quote, and writes reminders and thank-you notes. She can ask him to rewrite anything.
  • Makes changes when asked. "Add 10 guests to Johnny's dinner," and it's done.
  • Researches prices. He gives a typical price range for items at that kind of event.
  • Remembers. He can look up old quotes and the emails sent from the Atelier.
  • Shows the way. Step-by-step help stays on screen as she moves around the app.

Nothing goes to a client until she approves it. And he doesn't do wine pairings. That's her job, and she does it better.

Where it stands

Live, and sending real invoices to real clients. New features are tested on a demo login first, then moved to hers once they're simple enough.

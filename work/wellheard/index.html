WellHeard

Make every visit count.

Doctor's appointments are short and stressful. People forget their questions and leave unsure what was said. WellHeard is a calm voice that helps you get ready, records the visit, and gives you a plain-language recap to share with the people who help you.

Open WellHeard → Phone or computer. No sign-in.

How it works

  1. BeforeOne question at a time, at your pace. It suggests questions you wouldn't think to ask. You leave with one page for the doctor.
  2. DuringRecord the conversation.
  3. AfterAn editable recap of what was said and what happens next, ready to share.

Where it stands

A working prototype, built with one family and tested at real appointments. A doctor who saw it said, "He's hired."

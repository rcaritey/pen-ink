Appointment Prep

Get ready for your appointment

Answer a few questions, one at a time. At the end you'll have a one-page sheet to take to the doctor, and a list to share with family.

  • Takes about 15 minutes.
  • You can talk or type.
  • You can stop and come back. Your answers are saved on this phone or computer.
  • Helping a family member? Sit together and use their words.

You're ready

You have three pages, each for a different person.

  1. Summary for the doctor. Print it and hand it over when you sit down.
  2. My page. Print it for yourself. Read your questions from it and jot down what the doctor says.
  3. Full list for family. Send it to family who help.

How do you want to send it? It goes from your own email or phone. Nothing is stored on this website.

Keep a copy

Your answers are saved on this phone or computer, so you can come back any time. To keep them safe, or to use them on another device, download a copy. Keep it somewhere private.

Start a new one. Your health background and medications stay, so you don't have to type them again.

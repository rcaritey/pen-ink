Appointment prep

Get ready for the appointment

Work through it together, one step at a time. Nothing is saved: when you close this page, it's gone.

Before you begin

Say this first "We'll take this slowly. I'll ask one thing at a time, and I won't move on until you say 'carry on'."
If they go quiet Wait. After about 15 seconds, ask: "Should I continue, or do you need more time?"

For example: neurologist, family doctor, foot care clinic.

A regular check-up, or something specific? Use their words.

In their own words

Ask, then listen "What's on your mind about this appointment?" Let them talk it all out. No questions yet. Write down what they say, in their words.

What to tell them

Short facts for the doctor: what's happening, where, since when, what makes it better or worse.

What to ask

Their own questions first. Tap Top on the ones that matter most. Those go first on the sheet, marked with a star.

Questions they might not think of

Read each one out. Tick only the ones they want. They decide.

Medications
